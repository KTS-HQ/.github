## Who we are

KTS Technology Group is a UK-based **Managed Intelligence Provider (MIP)**, headquartered in Dorset in the South of England.

We are not a traditional MSP. Rather than waiting for things to break, we combine expert engineers, automation and AI-driven tooling to keep our clients' technology secure, managed and ahead of problems. Our clients are businesses that want IT that simply works, so their teams can focus on the work that matters.

## What we do

KTS operates across three divisions:

| Division | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **KTS Technology** | Managed IT, cyber security, cloud services, device management and automation |
| **KTS Broadband** | Business connectivity delivered over our own network |
| **KTS Telecoms** | Business telephony and voice systems |

### Our services

- **KTS Proactive Care**: fully managed IT support built around prevention, monitoring and fast resolution.
- **Intelligence as a Service**: automation and AI applied to real operational problems, from monitoring to reporting.
- **KTS Security Operations Centre**: continuous security monitoring, threat detection and response.
- **PatchCare+**: managed patching and endpoint management across Mac and Windows estates.
- **SafeCheck**: anti-phishing browser protection deployed and managed across client estates.

We specialise in **Apple-first businesses** (Mac, iPhone and iPad) running on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.

## How we work

- **Precision**: every system is documented, monitored and deliberately configured.
- **Simplicity**: complex technology, delivered in plain English.
- **Security by design**: privacy, UK GDPR compliance and data ownership are core requirements, never add-ons.
- **Automation with purpose**: we automate and self-host where it gives us and our clients meaningful control, not for its own sake.
- **Partnership**: we work as an extension of our clients' teams, not a helpdesk at arm's length.
